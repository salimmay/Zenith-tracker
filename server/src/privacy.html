🎬 Zenith Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

Zenith Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Zenith Tracker mobile application and API service. We believe in privacy by design: Zenith Tracker is fully functional without creating an account. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how your privacy is protected.

2. Information We Collect

A. Guest Mode (No Account Required)

If you use Zenith Tracker without creating an account, all your watchlist items, progress check-offs, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device. This data is never sent to our account databases unless you explicitly sign up to back up your library.

B. Account Registration (Optional)

If you choose to create an account to synchronize your watch history across multiple devices, we collect:

  • Username & Email: Used for authentication and account recovery.
  • Password: Stored strictly as a cryptographic hash (using bcrypt); we never have access to plain-text passwords.
  • Watch History & Library: Shows, movies, seasons, and episodes you track.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Logs

When connecting to our servers, standard diagnostic data (such as temporary IP addresses for rate limiting and app error reports) may be temporarily processed to ensure reliability and prevent abuse.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Advertising

A. The Movie Database (TMDB)

Zenith fetches public movie and TV metadata from TMDB. Your personal information, watch history, or email are never shared with TMDB.

B. Trakt.tv (Optional Sync)

If you voluntarily connect your Trakt account via OAuth device login, Zenith mirrors your watched episodes to your Trakt profile. You can disconnect Trakt anytime from the Settings screen.

C. Google AdMob (Advertisements)

On mobile platforms, Zenith displays banner ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) to serve non-personalized or personalized ads depending on your consent.

Consent Management: Users in the EEA/UK are presented with a Google-certified consent form. You can change your ad privacy preferences anytime via Settings → Ad privacy choices.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data exchanged between Zenith Tracker and our servers is encrypted using modern HTTPS (TLS) encryption. Account data is securely housed in MongoDB Atlas with encryption at rest and in transit.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You own your data. You may request deletion of your account and all associated watch history at any time directly in the app under Settings → Delete Account or by emailing us. All data will be permanently wiped from our systems.

6. Children's Privacy

Zenith Tracker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
